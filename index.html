01 / CASE STUDY↗

Transportation Procurement Strategy

NorthStar Beverage Group · Supply chain procurement class activity

In a fictional multinational beverage-company scenario, transportation procurement varied across plants and regions. I used a supply-risk-versus-business-impact framework to think through how categories such as domestic truckload and emergency airfreight should be managed.

My approach: Evaluate exposure and importance by category, identify sourcing priorities, and connect recommended initiatives to responsible owners and measurable KPIs such as on-time, in-full delivery (OTIF).

Takeaway: Effective procurement is about service reliability and supplier risk as well as price.

02 / COURSE PROJECT↗

Oil Production Data Analysis

Business statistics coursework · Spreadsheet analysis

I worked with an oil-production dataset in Excel as part of a statistics assignment, pairing spreadsheet analysis with a written explanation of the results.

My approach: Organize the observations, use spreadsheet formulas for numerical analysis, and develop a clear presentation of results while checking calculations against the provided dataset and assignment requirements.

Takeaway: Sound analysis depends on both accurate calculations and a clear explanation of what the data shows.

03 / SIMULATION↗

Walmart Area Manager Simulation

Walmart · Forage job simulation

I completed an operations-focused virtual job simulation and reflected on the decisions and expectations involved in an area management role.

My approach: Work through the scenarios, explain my reasoning, and reflect on operational priorities such as effective communication, problem solving, and balancing competing needs.

Takeaway: Good operations leadership requires a combination of analytical thinking, timely decisions, and attention to people.
